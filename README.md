# Life Matters · 立民

Life Matters is an academic simulation platform and card game focused on health decision-making and historical mortality scenarios.

## Medical & Historical Disclaimer

### Life Matters · 立民 — 历史场景免责声明

本游戏中的历史场景及人物模拟，基于公开学术文献与历史记录，仅用于健康决策教育目的。

- **所有模拟内容不代表对历史人物的道德评判**
- **历史数据经简化处理，不构成医学建议**
- **涉及真实人物的场景均以教育为目的，符合学术合理使用（Fair Use）原则**
- **游戏结果为模型推演，非历史事实重现**

---

**Life Matters** is an academic simulation tool for educational purposes only. Historical scenarios are based on publicly available literature and do not represent moral judgments of historical figures.

## Project Structure

- `sim_gui/`: The simulation platform frontend.
- `game/`: The card game frontend.
- `locales/`: Shared internationalization files.
- `mods/`: Scenario definitions (YAML).
